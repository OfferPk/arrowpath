How to play

  • Tap a glowing arrow — it flies in its facing direction.
  • Clear — if the path is empty to the edge, the arrow leaves the board.
  • Fail — hitting another arrow or a wall ends the try. Retry or Undo.
  • Order matters — clear blocking arrows first.
  • Hint — highlights a safe next arrow (rewarded stub).
